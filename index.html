About Dr. Ezeonu

Dr. Thelma Ezeonu serves as managing director of Hamthel Holdings. She founded Flirtshade Cosmetics in the United States, a beauty brand that makes products for a range of skin tones.

She co-founded the Hamilton & Thelma Ezeonu Foundation with Chief Hamilton Ezeonu. Its work includes education, healthcare outreach, youth empowerment and humanitarian relief in Nigeria.

Hamilton & Thelma Ezeonu Foundation

One Child, One School Bag
