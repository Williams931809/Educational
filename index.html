Biography

A career across medicine and business.

Dr. Ezeonu trained in medicine in the United States, where she also founded Flirtshade Cosmetics. She serves as managing director of Hamthel Holdings and co-founded the Hamilton & Thelma Ezeonu Foundation, whose school outreach has reached communities across Anambra.
