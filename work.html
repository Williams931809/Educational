Leadership and entrepreneurship

Dr. Ezeonu serves as managing director of Hamthel Holdings and founded Flirtshade Cosmetics in the United States.

Managing Director

Hamthel Holdings

Hamthel Holdings brings together businesses that serve different stages of property ownership, from real estate and building materials to the products people use in their homes. Its portfolio includes paints, smart locks, renewable energy, textiles and short-let accommodation.

The company also shares the Hamilton & Thelma Ezeonu Foundation’s outreach through its community impact programme.

Founder

Flirtshade Cosmetics

Dr. Ezeonu founded Flirtshade in the United States. The brand focuses on inclusive beauty, with cosmetics designed for customers across a range of skin tones. Its Beauty Bulb Live exhibitor profile presents it as a luxury beauty brand with a global audience.
