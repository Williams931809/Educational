About

Dr. Thelma Ezeonu

Dr. Ezeonu trained as a physician in the United States. Her work also spans business leadership, beauty and educational outreach.

Medicine, business and education

Hamthel Holdings identifies Dr. Ezeonu as its managing director. Her entrepreneurial work includes founding Flirtshade Cosmetics in the United States, a brand that develops beauty products for a range of skin tones.

As a co-founder of the Hamilton & Thelma Ezeonu Foundation, she has visited public schools during the foundation’s school-bag distributions. In Agulu, she urged children to take their education seriously and build the habits that will help them pursue their goals.
